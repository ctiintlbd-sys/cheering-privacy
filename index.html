Privacy Policy

App Name: Cr-Shop | Effective Date: October 2024

Welcome to Cheering ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of our users ("you," "customer"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use the Cheering mobile application.

By downloading, installing, or using Cheering, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a smooth shopping experience and order fulfillment, we collect several types of information:

A. Personal Information

  • Account Data: Name, email address, phone number, and password when you register an account.
  • Delivery & Shipping Information: Full delivery address, contact person name, apartment/building number, and postal code for delivering ordered products.
  • Order & Purchase History: Details of the items you view, add to cart, wishlist, and purchase.

B. Device & App Usage Data

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network info.
  • Log & Analytics Data: IP address, browser/app session details, crash reports, and performance analytics to improve app stability.

C. Device Permissions

  • Location: Used optionally to automatically detect or pinpoint your delivery address and locate nearby merchant stores.
  • Camera & Storage / Photos: Used if you wish to upload a profile picture, attach photos for product reviews, or send images for customer support inquiries.
  • Notifications: Used to send real-time push notifications regarding order updates, delivery status, and promotional deals.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To process, confirm, manage, and deliver your shopping orders.
  • To securely handle transactions and send electronic invoices or receipts.
  • To enable real-time order tracking and communicate delivery status updates.
  • To provide responsive customer support and resolve user complaints.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions and unauthorized app access.
  • To personalize your in-app recommendations and improve product discovery.

3. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share necessary details with trusted third parties under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Vendors & Merchants: Only the essential order information and delivery destination required to pack and fulfill your purchase.
  • Logistics & Delivery Partners: Your delivery address and phone number so delivery personnel can contact you and drop off your package.
  • Payment Processors: We partner with trusted payment gateways (e.g., bKash, Nagad, Cards) for secure checkout. We do not store sensitive credit card numbers or PINs directly on our servers.
  • Legal & Compliance: When required by applicable laws, court orders, or governmental regulations.

4. Data Retention and Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures (such as SSL/TLS encryption, secured databases, and access control) to protect your personal data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

We retain your information as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services and satisfy legal or accounting obligations.

5. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

You have full control over your data:

You have the right to review, update, or permanently delete your account and personal data at any time.

  • In-App Deletion: Navigate to Profile / Settings > Delete Account and follow the confirmation prompt.
  • Manual Request: Send an email to our support team at support@ctiintlbd.com with the subject line "Delete My Account" from your registered email address.

Upon request, your account data and personal records will be permanently removed or anonymized within 30 days, except records required by tax and financial laws.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal details, we promptly remove it from our servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our legal obligations or application features. When changes are made, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this document and notify users through the application if the modifications are significant.
