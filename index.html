Privacy Policy

App Name: Cheering (Customer App)

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

Official Website: https://www.cheeringshop.online/

Welcome to Cheering. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how our customer mobile application collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you browse, order, and purchase products from shops and vendors through our platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a smooth e-commerce shopping experience, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal Information Provided by You

  • Account Details: Full name, mobile phone number, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Delivery Information: Physical shipping/delivery address, apartment/house details, and recipient contact information.
  • Order Records: Products added to cart, purchase history, payment reference tokens, and special delivery instructions.
  • Customer Inquiries: Messages, complaints, ratings, and reviews shared with customer care or vendors.

B. Automatically Collected Information & App Permissions

  • Location Data: With your permission, we access your device's precise or approximate location to suggest local shops, calculate shipping fees, and facilitate delivery tracking.
  • Device & Log Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, network state, and app crash logs.
  • Push Notifications: Device tokens used to send timely order updates, vendor responses, promotional offers, and dispatch notices.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data to operate, improve, and secure our services, specifically to:

  • Manage and authenticate your Cheering account profile.
  • Process and fulfill orders, routing details to relevant sellers and delivery couriers.
  • Facilitate secure transaction processing and issue invoices/receipts.
  • Deliver critical status alerts regarding purchases and delivery progress.
  • Detect and prevent security issues, fraud, and abusive activities.
  • Enhance app features and deliver tailored product suggestions.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data is shared strictly under the following conditions:

  • Registered Vendors & Stores: Vendors receive ordered product lists, recipient names, and delivery addresses to prepare and dispatch items.
  • Logistics Partners: Riders and third-party courier services receive your shipping address and contact number solely to complete delivery.
  • Service Providers: Verified infrastructure providers, SMS gateways, and secure payment processing services operating under strict non-disclosure obligations.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by legal mandates, law enforcement, or applicable judicial processes.

4. Payment Security

Cheering does not process or store sensitive payment credentials (such as credit/debit card numbers or mobile banking PINs) on our systems. All transactions are securely routed through certified, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your profile and transaction records as long as your account remains active or as needed to comply with operational, tax, and legal requirements.

Account & Data Deletion: You can request permanent account deletion along with your associated personal records at any time. You may submit this request through the in-app profile settings or by sending an email directly to cheering.app.bd@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

6. Children's Privacy

Cheering is designed for general audiences and is not targeted at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If such data is identified, it is promptly removed from our databases.

7. Data Security Measures

We apply standard security protocols, including HTTPS encryption, restricted server access, and secure authentication tokens to defend your personal data from unauthorized access, modification, or exposure.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, technical upgrades, or statutory requirements. Changes become effective upon publication on this page.
